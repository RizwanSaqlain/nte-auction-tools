How to Use the Gold Finder

Enter the gadget’s total, adjust your limits, and add any known gold items. Every returned bundle matches that total and respects your selected constraints. Slot counts are summed; this tool does not check whether item shapes fit a box layout.

A search may stop at your result limit or after a time limit. Partial results are clearly marked and are not ranked by likelihood. The total only covers gold-rarity collectibles; other rarities may add value to the box.
